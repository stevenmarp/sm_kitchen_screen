Kitchen Screen

Realtime kitchen display for Odoo Point of Sale. Orders flow from the POS to the kitchen instantly, move through Cooking / Ready / Done, and customers follow their order number on a status screen.
Key Features

Realtime Updates

New POS orders appear on the kitchen display instantly over the Odoo bus. No refresh, no polling.

Multiple Screens

Main Kitchen, Bar, Dessert station: each screen has its own POS configurations and product categories.

Order Status Screen

Customer-facing screen with big green Ready and gray Cooking order numbers.

How To Use

  • Go to Point of Sale > Kitchen Screens and create a screen
  • Pick the POS configurations and product categories it should show
  • Click Open Screen on the kitchen display device
  • Click Order Status on the customer-facing device
Built For The Rush

Cooking, Ready, Done

A dark, glanceable display designed for busy kitchens.

Color-Coded Timers

Every order shows its waiting time: green under 10 minutes, amber under 20, pulsing red beyond.

Tick Off Lines

Tap a line to mark it prepared. Internal and customer notes show as badges next to the product.

Star & Print

Pin priority orders to the front with a star and print a preparation ticket per order.
